What is DEXTA?

DEXTA is your digital DECA companion designed to help you practice roleplays, receive AI-driven feedback, and simulate real judge evaluations. Built by students, for students, DEXTA blends AI grading, performance coaching, and conversational learning to elevate your competition prep.

How DEXTA Helps You Compete

  • ✔️ Practice Roleplays: Upload your event or presentation and get detailed scoring breakdowns.
  • ✔️ AI Evaluation: Understand where you excel and where to improve before competition day.
  • ✔️ Chat-Based Coaching: Ask DEXTA how to boost your strategy, script, or delivery.
